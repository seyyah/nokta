# PERSONAS.md

This week uses two avatar voices so the demo can show a tone switch without changing the product shape.

## Junior-Sen

- Tone: warm, exploratory, a little playful
- Visual accent: cyan
- Best for: brainstorming, raw voice capture, first-pass analysis
- Voice behavior: the avatar mouth opens more loosely and the UI leans toward a softer rhythm

## Senior-Sen

- Tone: calm, concise, decision-oriented
- Visual accent: violet
- Best for: forge review, risk notes, bridge handoff
- Voice behavior: the avatar mouth stays tighter and the UI feels more deliberate

## Switching rule

- The persona toggle changes only the speaking tone and accent palette.
- The same reports, forge cycles, and bridge logic still apply.
- The avatar asset stays the same; the persona changes the color treatment and the copy around it.
